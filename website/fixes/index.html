What LYNX fixes

Complaints that come up again and again about DAWs, and what LYNX SHARE does about each one. Everything in the list is in the current release.

Crashes and lost work

One plugin crash closes everything

The complaintIn most DAWs a third-party plugin runs inside the program. When the plugin crashes, the program goes with it, and so does anything you had not saved.

In LYNX SHARETurn on "Run plugins in a separate process" in Options › General settings, or choose it for one plugin from the menu of its window. A plugin that crashes then goes silent and the program keeps running. It is off by default, and such a plugin opens its editor in its own window. Even without it, a plugin that crashes while it plays is switched off and the program keeps running, on Windows, macOS and Linux. A plugin that crashed can be reloaded from its window, with its settings from the last save or backup, without reopening the project.

A plugin scan that hangs

The complaintOne broken plugin can freeze or crash the scan of a whole plugin folder, and you are left to find the culprit by hand.

In LYNX SHAREEach plugin file is scanned in its own process. A file that crashes or takes too long is marked as failed and skipped from then on. In the Plugin manager you can right-click it and try again.

Autosave that interrupts playback

The complaintA timed backup that lands while the song is playing can make the audio stutter.

In LYNX SHAREBackups wait while the song plays or while you play a MIDI keyboard, and none is written during a recording. After ten minutes without a backup one is made anyway. Both are settings in Options › General settings.

Finding your work after a crash

The complaintAfter a crash it is often unclear whether a backup exists and where it is.

In LYNX SHAREA backup copy is saved every two minutes and the ten newest are kept for each project. After an unclean exit, the next start offers the newest one. File › Revert to last backup and File › Open autosave folder reach them at any time. File › Versions... lists a project's saved versions and backups in one window.

The idea you played without recording

The complaintThe best take is often the one played before pressing record.

In LYNX SHARECapture MIDI (Ctrl+Shift+Alt+R) puts the notes you just played into the pattern. Tools › Audio logger keeps a rolling recording of the Master and is on by default.

A project that crashes while it opens

The complaintAfter a plugin update, a project can crash the DAW every time it loads, and the only way to find the culprit is to remove plugins one by one.

In LYNX SHAREFile › Open without third-party plugins... opens the project with every third-party plugin off and its settings kept. A window lists them, and Switch on brings them back one at a time, so the one that breaks the project shows itself.

Projects that live only on a server

The complaintSome online DAWs keep no copy of a project on your device. When the account, the connection or the service has a problem, the work is out of reach.

In LYNX SHAREA LYNX SHARE project is a file on your own disk and the program works offline. Nothing is uploaded unless you send a project to someone or start a live session.

Plugins

Old 32-bit plugins

The complaintMany 64-bit DAWs no longer load 32-bit plugins, so older instruments and effects are lost.

In LYNX SHAREOn Windows, 32-bit VST2 and VST3 plugins run through a bridge that adds no latency. This is Windows only, and the plugin's editor opens in its own window.

Sidechain takes too many steps

The complaintFeeding one track into another track's compressor usually means building the route by hand in the mixer. In FL Studio that is several steps.

In LYNX SHAREEffects with a side input have a "Sidechain from" picker that lists the inserts. Picking one makes the route. Third-party plugins with a second input get the same picker.

Which plugin is using the CPU

The complaintWhen the audio crackles it is hard to tell which plugin is responsible.

In LYNX SHARETools › Plugin performance monitor lists every instrument and effect with its processing time now, on average and at its peak.

CLAP plugins

The complaintCLAP is a newer, open plugin format, and not every DAW loads it.

In LYNX SHARELYNX SHARE loads CLAP plugins on Windows, macOS and Linux, next to VST3. VST2, Audio Units and LV2 load on the systems that have them.

Heavy instruments that use up the computer

The complaintA project with many heavy instruments reaches the limit of the computer's memory and processor. Bouncing each one to audio by hand loses the way back.

In LYNX SHARERight-click a channel in the Channel rack and choose Freeze. The channel plays audio rendered from it and its instrument is unloaded, a third-party plugin included. Unfreeze brings back exactly what was there. Only instrument channels can be frozen, not mixer inserts.

Plugin windows that slip behind

The complaintA plugin window you are tweaking disappears behind the main window as soon as you click elsewhere.

In LYNX SHAREA detached plugin window has Pin on top in its header and in its menu, and then stays above LYNX SHARE's other windows. It is not available for a plugin in a separate process.

Splitting a song into stems costs extra

The complaintStem separation is in the most expensive edition of some DAWs and behind a membership in others.

In LYNX SHARERight-click an audio clip in the Playlist and choose Extract stems from sample: drums, bass, vocals and the other instruments come out on new Playlist tracks. It runs on your own computer; the model is downloaded once, after asking.

AI tools that send your project away

The complaintAssistants built into DAWs upload project data to the maker's servers, and it is not always clear what is sent.

In LYNX SHAREConvert to MIDI and Extract stems from sample run on your own computer. LYNX SHARE has no built-in assistant and no account, and sends no project data unless you start a live session, send a project or agree to send a crash report.

Moving between DAWs

Projects tied to one program

The complaintFL Studio and Ableton Live cannot open each other's projects. Moving a song usually means exporting audio and starting again.

In LYNX SHAREFile › Open reads .flp, .als (Live 11 and 12) and .flm. File › Export writes an FL Studio project or an Ableton Live set. A report lists what was approximated. In Live sets, Live's own devices are not converted.

Missing samples after a project moves

The complaintA project opened on another computer, or after its files were moved, loses its samples and gives little help in finding them.

In LYNX SHAREThe Missing files window opens after loading. It can search a folder, locate one file or skip it. Help › Missing files opens it again.

Routing that breaks when mixer tracks move

The complaintIn FL Studio, moving or deleting mixer tracks can leave sends and sidechains pointing at the wrong track.

In LYNX SHAREMoving, inserting or deleting mixer tracks keeps sends, sidechains, automation and MIDI links on the track they belong to. Links to a track you delete can be kept and shown greyed, and the inserts can be put in the order of the Playlist's tracks.

Recording and export

Recorded notes you cannot see until you stop

The complaintIn FL Studio, the notes you are recording do not appear in the Playlist until the recording ends.

In LYNX SHAREThe take is drawn in the Playlist and the Piano roll while you record.

Stems with the bus effects baked in

The complaintStem exports often carry send and bus effects that the person receiving them does not want.

In LYNX SHAREThe export window has "One per channel, instruments only (no sends or buses)".

Hitting a loudness target

The complaintReaching a streaming target such as -14 LUFS takes several trial exports in a DAW that has no loudness target.

In LYNX SHAREFile › Export › Master (Ctrl+Alt+M) masters the song to the LUFS target you choose, on your own computer. A loudness meter beside the Master (off by default) shows short-term and integrated LUFS and the true peak. View › Reference match... measures a finished song you like and matches the Master's tone and loudness to it.

Small things FL Studio users keep asking for

The complaintFiner knob movements, a gain-reduction meter on the mixer strip and tools to clean up unused patterns are long-standing requests.

In LYNX SHARECtrl+drag moves a control finely and Ctrl+Shift+drag a hundred times finer. Mixer strips show gain reduction for LYNX SHARE's own dynamics effects. The pattern menu has Select unused, Delete unused patterns and Merge identical patterns.

Audio that is not in time

The complaintA recorded loop or a sample at another tempo has to be lined up with the project by hand.

In LYNX SHAREAuto-warp finds the beat of an audio clip and locks it to the project's tempo. In the audio editor's warp lane you can add, move and remove the markers yourself.

Getting the notes out of a recording

The complaintTurning a hummed melody or a drum loop into notes usually takes another program or an online service.

In LYNX SHAREConvert to MIDI turns the melody, the harmony or the drums of an audio clip into a new pattern. It runs on your own computer.

Undo keys that differ between programs

The complaintIn FL Studio, Ctrl+Z undoes and redoes only the last step. People who come from other programs expect it to keep stepping back.

In LYNX SHAREBy default the keys are FL Studio's: Ctrl+Z undoes and redoes the last step, and Ctrl+Alt+Z steps back through the history. "Alternate undo mode" in the general settings swaps the two.

Too many windows on the first day

The complaintA full DAW shows a beginner dozens of windows and controls before the first sound.

In LYNX SHAREView › Layout style › Simple workflow shows one window with the tracks and the timeline. A new track is a voice, an instrument, a sampler or a starter beat in one click, and the other windows stay one click away. The setup wizard offers it at the first start.

Hearing yourself late while you record

The complaintAn effect that adds latency on the track you record through, or on the Master, makes you hear your own voice or playing late.

In LYNX SHARERight-click the record button, or open Mixer › Disk recording, and turn on Low latency while recording. Effects that add latency on an armed or monitored insert, and on its way to the Master, are bypassed until the insert is disarmed or its monitoring is turned off. What is recorded does not change. It is off by default.

Loops that preview at the wrong tempo

The complaintAuditioning a loop in the browser plays it at its own tempo, so you cannot tell whether it fits the song.

In LYNX SHARETurn on Preview in time in the Browser's menu: loops then play at the project's tempo and on the beat. It is off by default.

Notes that slip out of key

The complaintDrawing or moving notes by hand easily lands them outside the song's scale.

In LYNX SHAREChoose a scale in the Piano roll and turn on Snap to scale: new and moved notes, and notes coming in from a MIDI keyboard, land on the scale.

Exporting every section of a song, again and again

The complaintRendering the intro, verse and drop as separate files, with the same settings each time, means setting up the export window over and over.

In LYNX SHAREThe Export window has One file per marker section, which renders each section between Playlist markers to its own file, also with stems. Its settings can be saved as named presets.

Sends that move with the fader

The complaintHeadphone mixes and some effects need a send that keeps its level when the track's fader moves.

In LYNX SHARERight-click a send knob or a route arrow in the Mixer and choose Pre-fader send. FL Studio's post-fader send stays the default.

No way to comp the best take

The complaintBuilding one take from the best parts of several means cutting, moving and muting clips by hand.

In LYNX SHARELoop recording keeps every pass on its own Playlist track in a group. Right-click a track of the group and choose Comp these takes: a comp track is added above them, a drag across a take sends that part to it, with short crossfades where the take changes, and Flatten comp keeps what plays.

Recordings that land a little late

The complaintA take lands a few milliseconds behind the beat, and it is hard to tell whether the DAW or the audio interface is to blame.

In LYNX SHARERecordings are placed using the latency the audio driver reports. For an exact figure, Options › Audio settings... › Measure the round trip plays clicks from an output to an input through a cable or a loopback and times their way back. With Use the measured round trip on, takes are placed by that figure, kept for that device, sample rate and buffer size.

Price and systems

No Linux version

The complaintFL Studio and Ableton Live are not made for Linux.

In LYNX SHARELYNX SHARE has a native Linux build (AppImage and tar.gz) with PipeWire, JACK and ALSA, and loads VST3, CLAP, LV2 and VST2 plugins there. It needs X11 or XWayland.

Trials, accounts and subscriptions

The complaintTrial versions often cannot reopen the projects you saved, and many programs need an account or a subscription.

In LYNX SHARELYNX SHARE is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0, with no account. Saving and reopening have no limit. Crash reports are sent only if you agree, and the update check sends nothing about you.
