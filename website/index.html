A lightweight DAW, fully open source.

LYNX SHARE is a DAW that works the way FL Studio does: Channel rack, Piano roll, Playlist and Mixer, with the same shortcuts. It opens FL Studio projects (.flp), Ableton Live sets (.als) and FL Studio Mobile projects (.flm), and exports .flp and .als back. It is free under the AGPL-3.0 and still an alpha.

What it does

You build patterns in the Channel rack and the Piano roll, arrange them with audio and automation clips in the Playlist, and mix in the Mixer: up to 127 insert tracks with 10 effect slots each, sends and sidechain. If you know FL Studio, the windows, mouse gestures and shortcuts are where you expect them.

Projects from other programs open directly. An .flp comes in with its channels, samples, patterns, arrangement, automation and mixer, and FL Studio's native plugins are replaced by LYNX SHARE's own engines. Live 11 and 12 sets open too, and their Session clips land in an optional clip grid with scenes. The same menu (View › Layout style) has a Simple workflow: one window for a first song.

Third-party plugins load as VST3 and CLAP on every system, plus Audio Units on macOS and LV2 on Linux. VST2 plugins load on Windows and Linux, and on Windows 32-bit plugins run through a bridge.

What is missing

It is an alpha, so expect bugs and keep backups. Not every FL Studio plugin is recreated exactly: the plugin list gives the status of each one. Only some of Live's own devices are converted, and in .flm projects only some of FL Studio Mobile's instruments are recreated. The Windows build is not code-signed and the macOS build is not notarized yet, so both systems warn the first time you open it.

Effects closer to the originals, more of FL Studio, Session mixer and Drum Rack, a first-song tour

LYNX SHARE's reverbs, delays, modulation effects, dynamics and voice tools were refitted against the plugins they are modelled on, and projects saved earlier keep their sound. More of FL Studio's windows and menus are in, the Clip-launch workflow gains a Session mixer, a Drum Rack and map modes, many of Live's own devices now open from .als sets, FL Studio Mobile projects gain DW Sampler and Slicer, and the Simple workflow gets an Effects tab and a first-song tour. LYNX SHARE also has a new logo, by w3teal.

  • Effects closer to the originals: Epoch Verb, Atrium, Foil Plate, Halo, Horizon and Nebula, Orbit, Spiral Echo, Lattice Mod, Gemini Doubler and Echoform have a measured law, and Sentry, Hush, Glow, Voice Shift and Quell gained controls measured on the plugins they are modelled on. Orbit's tail score went from 6.32 to 8.27 and Nebula's from 5.84 to 8.43 (out of 10). New instances use the measured law; projects and presets saved earlier load the classic one and sound exactly as before.
  • Remote control settings, as in FL Studio: right-click a control › Link to controller... opens the window with the port, channel, controller, the mapping formula, smoothing and FL's Mode list, for knobs, notes and buttons. Automation clips have FL's 13 curve modes per point, and the Piano roll gains Limit, Scale levels, Flip and Randomize tools.
  • Edison on a mixer slot, as in FL Studio: it records now, on input or on play, keeps recording with its window closed and comes in from FL projects with its audio. Tools › One-click audio recording records into Edison or the Playlist.
  • The Sampler page in FL Studio's layout: Content, Playback with loop points, ping pong and start offset, Time stretching, Precomputed effects with crossfade and trim, and the waveform. LYNX SHARE's own controls are in a last box. (#156)

Source code

LYNX SHARE is written in C++20 with JUCE 8. The code, the tests and the notes on every FL Studio behaviour it reproduces are in one public repository. FL Studio and Live were studied from the outside and the code was written from scratch: nothing from Image-Line or Ableton is in it.

Bug reports and pull requests are welcome. Several fixes in recent releases came from users.

Crash reports

Opt-in only. After a crash or freeze LYNX SHARE asks before sending a report (Settings › General › Crash reports: Ask / Always send / Never send). A report holds a random id, the LYNX SHARE version, the operating system, CPU, graphics cards and drivers, screen size, audio device and settings, the crash or freeze file, the session's last 60 steps, and the names and versions of the third-party plugins that were loaded. User names in paths become <user>, and file names are left out unless you tick "include file names". No audio, projects or samples, no machine name and no IP address are stored. Reports are used only to fix bugs. To have one deleted, open an issue with its id (LYNX SHARE shows it after sending; every sent id is also listed in crash-reports/sent.log in LYNX SHARE's settings folder).
