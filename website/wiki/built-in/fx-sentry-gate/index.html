Sentry Gate

Gate and expander with Classic, Clean, Vocal, Guitar and Upward styles, plus hold, knee, look-ahead and side-chain filters, drawn over a level display.

EffectDynamics

On this page

  1. Controls
  2. Tips
  3. Related

Sentry Gate is a native LYNX SHARE gate and expander for a mixer insert. It cuts the signal below a threshold, from a gentle expansion to a full gate depending on ratio and range. The display shows the transfer curve over the live level.

Controls

ControlWhat it does
StyleClassic, Clean, Vocal, Guitar or Upward. Changes how the gate opens and closes.
ThresholdLevel at which the gate opens, from -60 dB to 0 dB.
RatioFrom 1:1 up to 100:1, expander to gate.
RangeHow far the signal is reduced, from 0 to 100 dB.
AttackOpening time, from 0 ms to 1 s.
ReleaseClosing time, from 0 ms to 5 s.
HoldHow long the gate stays open, from 0 to 250 ms.
KneeSoftness of the threshold, from 0 to 30 dB.
LookaheadFrom 0 to 10 ms, with an On switch.
Side chainInternal or external, with a high pass and a low pass filter.
Channel modeMenu in the bottom bar (Left/Right in the picture).
Oversampling, In and OutOversampling menu and input and output gain.
ModelClassic or Measured. Measured adds the Vocal style's noise reduction; the other styles ignore it. Projects saved before 0.1.26 load on Classic.
TriggerLevel or Transient. Level is the gate as before. Transient is a drum gate: a hit above the threshold opens it fully, it stays open for Hold and closes over Release along the chosen shape, whatever the level does meanwhile. Ratio, Knee and the style's curve are not used in Transient. Level by default.
ShapeTransient only. The shape of the release: Smooth, Mid or Sharp.
SensitivityTransient only. 1 to 10, default 5. Higher values open the gate on smaller hits.

Tips

  • Turning Lookahead on adds 10 ms of latency.
