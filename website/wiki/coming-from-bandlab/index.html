Coming from BandLab, FL Studio Mobile or GarageBand

The Simple layout for people who make music on a phone, a tablet or in the browser: what is the same, what maps to what, and opening your projects.

On this page

  1. Your first three things
  2. What is the same
  3. What is different

  6. Good to know
  7. Related

Pick the Simple layout. It is one window with your tracks on a timeline, loops, a drum machine and quick recording, the way BandLab, FL Studio Mobile and GarageBand work. The other two layouts stay one click away.

Your first three things

  1. Pick the layout. The first time LYNX SHARE starts, a welcome card asks where you are coming from. Click BandLab, FL Studio Mobile or GarageBand: the window switches to the Simple layout with a starter beat on a Drums track. Later, View › Layout style › Simple layout switches to it at any time.
  2. Make a beat. Click the squares of the Drum Machine under the timeline. See Make a beat in the Simple layout.
  3. Add a loop and your voice. Loops opens the loop panel: drag a loop onto the timeline. Then record your voice on a Voice track.

What is the same

  • Tracks on a timeline, one per voice or instrument, each with volume, pan, mute, solo and effects.
  • A drum machine with a step grid, loops that follow the song's tempo and key, and recording on a voice track.
  • One-knob effects, and a finished sound for the whole song before you save it.
  • Making music with friends: an online session puts up to 4 people in one project.

What is different

  • LYNX SHARE is a desktop program for Windows, macOS and Linux. It does not run in a browser or on a phone.
  • Your projects are .lynx files on your computer. There is no account and no cloud: save, back up and send them like any other file.
  • Third-party plugins load (VST3 and CLAP, Audio Units on macOS, LV2 on Linux, VST2 on Windows and Linux), next to LYNX SHARE's own instruments and effects.
  • When you want more, the Pattern layout (FL Studio's way of working, with the Channel rack and the Piano roll) and the Mixer (F9) work on the same project.

Your projects

  • FL Studio Mobile (.flm): File › Open... opens it. The import is partial: tempo, tracks, patterns, audio clips, drum kits, the DW Sampler, the Slicer, MiniSynth, Transistor Bass, 3x Osc, GMSynth, SuperSaw and FLM's effects come over. Other FLM instruments play LYNX SHARE's built-in synth. File › Save a copy as FL Studio Mobile project (.flm)... writes a copy back for FL Studio Mobile.
  • GarageBand (.band, from GarageBand 10 or GarageBand for iOS): File › Open... opens it as Simple-layout tracks with their regions, tempo, key and mix. Instrument tracks play LYNX SHARE's built-in synth and drums, because GarageBand does not store their sounds in a form other programs can read.
  • BandLab (.blx, a project downloaded by Cakewalk or Sonar): File › Open... opens it as Simple-layout tracks with regions, tempo, key, the mix, volume and pan automation and the common effects. BandLab's instrument sounds and other effects are listed in the import report.
  • Stems from any app: export the tracks as audio or MIDI files, put them in one folder, then click + Add track, and Folder... beside Import. Each file becomes a track that starts at the beginning.

Help › Last project import report lists what was approximated or left out. See File formats.

Good to know

  • File › Save writes a .lynx file. The file you opened is never changed.
  • Finish saves the song as WAV or MP3, and File › Export has more formats and stems. See Exporting.
  • Help › First-song tour walks you through a first song in about five minutes.
