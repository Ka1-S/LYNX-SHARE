`View › Layout style › Simple layout` shows one window with the tracks and the timeline. It mixes BandLab's approachable one-window style with FL Studio Mobile's way of working (tracks, loops, pads and quick recording), and it suits a first song. FL Studio Mobile projects (`.flm`) open in it, as in the other layouts. At the first start, the welcome card opens it when you say you are coming from BandLab, FL Studio Mobile, GarageBand or another timeline DAW, or are new to making music.

## What is in it

- **New track**: a chooser adds a voice, guitar or bass track to record on, an instrument that plays in one click, a sampler, or a starter beat placed for four bars.
- **Loops**: an audio loop dropped on the timeline keeps time with the project and moves to the project's key (`Follow the project key`, per clip).
- **Key**: the project has a key, and `Guess from notes` finds it from the notes of the song.
- **Finish**: the Finish dock gives the Master a ready-made sound and saves the song as WAV, MP3 or a package for other apps.
- **Track dock**: under the timeline, the selected track's Instrument, Keyboard, Editor and Effects tabs. The Effects tab has eight one-knob effects, 22 chain presets, a Blend knob, `Reverb` and `Echo` sends and `Duck under`, which dips the track under another one (the bass under the drums, say). The Keyboard tab plays keys, scale keys or a chord pad.
- **Sections**: `Sections` in the title bar marks the song's parts (intro, verse, chorus) on a lane under the ruler. A section moves, copies and repeats as a whole, with its time signature.
- **Time signature**: the top bar sets the song's time signature, and a right-click on the ruler (`Change time signature here...`) changes it from a bar on.
- **First song**: the Welcome screen offers a seven-step first-song tour, a demo song or an empty project (`Help › Welcome screen...`).

## Good to know

- Nothing is taken away: the Channel rack, Piano roll, Playlist and Mixer stay one click away.
- The same menu switches back to the Pattern layout at any time.

See [Layouts](/wiki/layouts/) for the other two.
