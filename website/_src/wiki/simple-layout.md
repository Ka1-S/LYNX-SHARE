`View › Layout style › Simple workflow` shows one window with the tracks and the timeline. It is meant for a first song. The setup wizard offers it at the first start, next to the Pattern workflow (the default) and the Clip-launch workflow.

## What is in it

- **New track**: a chooser adds a voice, guitar or bass track to record on, an instrument that plays in one click, a sampler, or a starter beat placed for four bars.
- **Loops**: an audio loop dropped on the timeline keeps time with the project.
- **Key**: the project has a key, and `Guess from notes` finds it from the notes of the song.
- **Finish**: the Finish dock gives the Master a ready-made sound and saves the song as WAV, MP3 or a package for other apps.

## Good to know

- Nothing is taken away: the Channel rack, Piano roll, Playlist and Mixer stay one click away.
- The same menu switches back to the Pattern workflow at any time.

See [Layouts](/wiki/layouts/) for the other two.
