---
title: The Device view
summary: In the Clip-launch layout, the Device view shows a track's instrument and effects in a row, as in Live: add effects from the Browser, open a device's window, copy devices between tracks.
---
## Show a track's devices

![The Device view of the Chords track](/assets/img/learn/device-view-1.webp)

1. In the Clip-launch layout, press `Ctrl+Alt+4` to show the Device view under the grid.
2. Click a track's title: its instrument comes first, then its effects, in the order the sound goes through them.

## Add effects

![An effect dragged from the Browser onto the chain](/assets/img/learn/device-view-2.webp)

1. In the Browser, click `Audio Effects` and drag an effect onto the chain, where you want it.

## Edit, copy, switch

![A device's own window](/assets/img/learn/device-view-3.webp)

1. Each device shows its main knobs; its window button opens the full editor.
2. Select a device and press `Ctrl+C`, then `Ctrl+V` on another track, to copy it.
3. `Shift+Tab` switches between the Device view and the clip.

See also [The three layouts](/wiki/layouts/) and [Tour the Clip-launch layout](/learn/clip-launch-tour/).
