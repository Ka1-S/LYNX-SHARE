title: Build a song with sections
summary: Name the parts of your song with the Sections lane, then copy or move a whole part, with everything under it, in one drag.
---
## Add sections

![The Sections button and the first section](/assets/img/learn/song-sections-1.webp)

1. In the Simple layout, click `Sections` in the title bar. The first section appears, covering the start of the song.
2. Click `+` at the left of the Sections lane to add the next one, eight bars long, after the last.
3. Sections take GarageBand's names in order: Intro, Verse, Chorus... Double-click one to rename it.

## Move and copy a whole part

![A section copied with Ctrl+drag](/assets/img/learn/song-sections-2.webp)

1. Drag a section to move it with every clip under it. A line shows where it goes.
2. Hold `Ctrl` while you drag to copy it instead. Drop it onto another section to swap the two.
3. Drag a section's right edge to change its length; the clips stay where they are.

## The section menu

![The menu of a section](/assets/img/learn/song-sections-3.webp)

- Right-click a section for `Rename...`, `Duplicate`, `Loop this section`, `Select its clips` and the delete commands.
- Right-click the lane for `Add a section at the end`, `Sections from markers` and `Create scenes from sections` (for the Clip-launch layout).

In the Pattern layout the Sections lane is a LYNX extra: switch it on in `Options › LYNX extras...`.

See also [Simple layout](/wiki/simple-layout/).
