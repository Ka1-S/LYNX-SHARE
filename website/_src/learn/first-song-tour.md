## Start the tour

![The welcome card with "I'm new to making music"](/assets/img/learn/first-song-tour-1.webp)

1. On the first start, LYNX SHARE asks where you are coming from. Choose `I'm new to making music`.
2. The Simple layout opens and the tour's card shows step 1 of 7. Each step points at the control to use.
3. Do the step yourself, or click `Do it for me`. `Next ›` moves on without doing it, `‹ Back` goes back.

## Beat, key and instrument

![Step 2: the song's key](/assets/img/learn/first-song-tour-2.webp)

1. **A beat**: `+ Add track`, then Drums, then a starter beat. Press Space to hear it.
2. **The key**: click `Key` on the toolbar and pick one. Chords and loops follow it.
3. **An instrument**: `+ Add track`, then Instrument: keys or a bass you can play at once.

## Chords, loop, effect, finish

![The finished first song](/assets/img/learn/first-song-tour-3.webp)

1. **Chords**: in the track's Keyboard tab choose `Chord pad` and record a few chords, or click notes into the Editor tab.
2. **A voice or a loop**: record your voice on a Voice track, or add a loop from `Loops`: it is moved to the song's key.
3. **An effect**: in a track's Effects tab add Room or Echo and turn its knob.
4. **Finish**: click `Finish` and pick a look for the whole song. Then save it as a file, or open a demo song to see how one is built.

Closing the card pauses the tour; `Help` brings it back. See also [Your first song](/wiki/first-song/).
