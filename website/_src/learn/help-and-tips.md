## Hints and tips

![A window's one-time tip](/assets/img/learn/help-and-tips-1.webp)

1. Point at any control: the hint bar at the top left says what it is and what it does, with its shortcut. Resting on a toolbar icon also shows its name.
   - In the Clip-launch layout the Info View at the bottom left shows the same help with a title; `?` shows or hides it, as in Live.
   - `Options › Toolbar › Hint bar (extended hint panel)` floats a larger hint panel you can move anywhere.
2. The first time you open some windows, such as the Mixer or the Piano roll, a tip shows once. `Got it` closes it, `No more tips` turns them all off; `Settings › General › Show tips and hints` brings them back. At most one tip shows per session.

## How to use this window

![The Mixer's How to use guide](/assets/img/learn/help-and-tips-2.webp)

1. The menu of each main window (the arrow at its top left: Playlist, Channel rack, Piano roll, Mixer, Browser and the others) ends with `How to use this...`: a short guide to the window and its first steps. `Help › How to use...` lists them all.
2. `Watch the tutorial` opens the window's video on lynx-share.com.
3. The `Help` menu also has `Getting started`, the `First-song tour`, `Tutorials (lynx-share.com/learn)...`, `Manual (wiki)...`, `FAQ and troubleshooting...` and `What's new in this version...`.
4. Something went wrong? `Help › Report a problem...` sends a report; after a crash, the next start asks whether to send one.

## Find any setting

![A setting found in the command palette](/assets/img/learn/help-and-tips-3.webp)

1. Press `?` for the command palette (`Help › Search actions...`) and type: it lists commands, menu items, the LYNX extras and now every option of Settings. In the Clip-launch layout `?` shows the Info View, as in Live: there, press `Ctrl+Shift+Y`.
2. Pick an option: Settings opens on its page, with the option outlined.

See also [Find any command](/learn/command-palette/) and [Settings](/wiki/settings/).
