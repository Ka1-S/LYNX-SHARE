---
## Add an EQ

![Facet EQ on the chords' track](/assets/img/learn/eq-basics-1.webp)

1. In the Mixer, select the track and click an empty slot, then choose `Facet EQ`.
2. The graph shows the sound. Double-click the graph to add a band.

## Cut and boost

![A low cut and a cut around 300 Hz](/assets/img/learn/eq-basics-2.webp)

1. Make a band near 80 Hz a `Low cut`: it removes the rumble under the bass.
2. Add a band near 300 Hz and drag it down a little: less mud.
3. A small lift near 3 kHz brings the notes forward. Drag a band up or down for its gain, left or right for its frequency; the wheel sets its width.

## Compare

![The listen switch: EQ and Source](/assets/img/learn/eq-basics-3.webp)

1. Click `Source` to hear the track without the EQ, at the same loudness; `EQ` brings it back.
2. Small moves are enough. Listen more than you look.
