---
## Choose where you are coming from

![The welcome card asks where you are coming from](/assets/img/learn/switch-layouts-1.webp)

1. Start LYNX SHARE. The first time, a welcome card asks where you are coming from.
2. Each answer picks a layout:
   - `BandLab, FL Studio Mobile or GarageBand`: the Simple layout, one window with tracks, loops and quick recording.
   - `FL Studio`: the Pattern layout, with the Channel rack, Piano roll, Playlist and Mixer.
   - `Ableton Live or Bitwig`: the Clip-launch layout, with a grid of clips and scenes.
3. Click one. Here we pick BandLab: the Simple layout opens with a starter beat on a Drums track.

## Switch the layout

![View › Layout style](/assets/img/learn/switch-layouts-2.webp)

1. Choose `View › Layout style`.
2. Pick `Pattern layout (default)`, `Clip-launch layout` or `Simple layout`.
3. The window changes; the project does not. The starter beat is now a pattern in the Channel rack and a clip in the Playlist.

![The same song in the Pattern layout](/assets/img/learn/switch-layouts-3.webp)

## The same song in Clip-launch

![The same song in the Clip-launch layout](/assets/img/learn/switch-layouts-4.webp)

1. Choose `View › Layout style › Clip-launch layout`.
2. The Playlist's tracks are the columns of the clip grid, and the arrangement sits beside it.
3. Press Play. All three layouts play and save the same project.

## Good to know

- LYNX SHARE remembers the layout you used last.
- `Help › Welcome...` shows the welcome card again.

See also [Layouts](/wiki/layouts/) and [Simple layout](/wiki/simple-layout/).
