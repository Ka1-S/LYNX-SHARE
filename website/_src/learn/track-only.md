## Patterns or tracks

![The Channel rack of a new project](/assets/img/learn/track-only-1.webp)

1. In FL Studio, instruments sit in the Channel rack and play patterns that you place in the Playlist.
2. If you prefer tracks, as in most other programs, turn on Track-only mode.

## Turn on Track-only mode

![Track-only mode in the LYNX extras](/assets/img/learn/track-only-2.webp)

1. Open `Options › LYNX extras...`, press `Ctrl+F` and type "track-only", then click the switch. `View › Layout style` has it too.
2. LYNX offers each instrument a track of its own: click `Yes`. One undo step takes it back.

## Draw clips on tracks

![Clips drawn on instrument tracks](/assets/img/learn/track-only-3.webp)

1. Click on a track to draw a clip: it plays that track's instrument. Double-click a clip to write its notes.
2. `F6` still opens the Channel rack, with the same instruments, each playing through its track's mixer track.

See also [Playlist](/wiki/playlist/) and [The three layouts](/wiki/layouts/).
