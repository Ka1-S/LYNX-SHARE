## Open the song

![File › Open...](/assets/img/learn/open-mobile-1.webp)

1. Copy the song to your computer: an FL Studio Mobile `.flm`, a BandLab project downloaded as `.blx`, or a GarageBand `.band`.
2. Choose `File › Open...` (`Ctrl+O`) and pick it, or drop it on the window.

## What you get

![The song in the Simple layout](/assets/img/learn/open-mobile-2.webp)

- The song opens in the Simple layout, one track per instrument, with the tempo, the patterns or regions and the mix.
- Press Play to hear it.
- Click a track to edit it in the dock under the timeline.

![A track in the dock](/assets/img/learn/open-mobile-3.webp)

## What could not come over

![The import report after opening the song](/assets/img/learn/open-mobile-4.webp)

- When something had to change, a short report opens with the song. Here, FL Studio Mobile's MiniSynth plays one of LYNX SHARE's synth sounds.
- `Help › Last project import report` shows that report again.
- Instruments the phone app keeps to itself play on LYNX SHARE's built-in sounds.

## Back to the phone

- `File › Save a copy as FL Studio Mobile project (.flm)...` writes a `.flm` you can open in FL Studio Mobile.

See also [Coming from BandLab, FL Studio Mobile or GarageBand](/wiki/coming-from-bandlab/) and [File formats](/wiki/file-formats/).
