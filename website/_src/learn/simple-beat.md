## Choose the Simple layout

![The welcome card asks where you are coming from](/assets/img/learn/simple-beat-1.webp)

1. Start LYNX SHARE. The first time, a welcome card asks where you are coming from.
2. Click `BandLab, FL Studio Mobile or GarageBand`. The window switches to the Simple layout, and a starter beat is placed on a Drums track.

Without the card, choose `View › Layout style › Simple layout`, click `+ Add track`, then `Drums`, and pick one of the starter beats. The same menu switches layouts at any time. The project stays as it is.

## Play the beat

![The Simple layout with the starter beat on a Drums track](/assets/img/learn/simple-beat-2.webp)

1. Press Play in the toolbar, or the space bar.
2. Press it again to stop.

The dock under the timeline shows the selected track. For a Drums track it opens on the `Drum Machine` tab.

## Change the beat

![The Drum Machine: one row per sound, one square per step](/assets/img/learn/simple-beat-3.webp)

1. Click a grey square of the Drum Machine to add a hit there. Click a lit square to remove it.
2. Each row is one sound of the kit: kick, snare, hats, clap and percussion.
3. `Length` sets how many bars the pattern has, and `Swing` moves every second step later.

The letter beside each row is its key on the computer keyboard: press it to play that sound.

## Set the tempo

![The tempo in the toolbar](/assets/img/learn/simple-beat-4.webp)

1. Drag the tempo up or down, or double-click it and type a number.
2. Play the beat again to hear it at the new speed.

## Next steps

![The Loops panel next to the timeline](/assets/img/learn/simple-beat-5.webp)

- The `Next: 3 things to try` card ticks each step off by itself. `Open Loops` opens the Loops panel: drag a loop onto the timeline. A loop follows the project's tempo and key.
- [Record your voice](/learn/record-voice/) on a Voice track.
- `Finish` saves the song as WAV or MP3.
