## The command palette

![The command palette with "tracker" typed](/assets/img/learn/command-palette-1.webp)

1. Press `?` (or `Ctrl+Shift+Y`, or click the magnifier in the toolbar).
2. Type a few letters of what you want. The list holds every action with its shortcut, every menu item with its place, the LYNX extras' switches, the Settings pages and your recent projects. Actions are found by their English names in every language too.
3. Pick a row with the arrow keys and press `Enter`, or double-click it. `Esc` closes the palette.

## Watch the CPU

![The CPU meter in the toolbar](/assets/img/learn/command-palette-2.webp)

1. The CPU meter in the toolbar shows how much of the audio time the project uses while it plays.
2. Double-click it to open the Plugin performance monitor: the load of every plugin and channel, now, on average and at its peak.

![The Plugin performance monitor](/assets/img/learn/command-palette-3.webp)

## Let plugins rest

- `Tools › Switch smart disable for all plugins` lets every plugin pause while it gets silence and makes no sound, as FL Studio's Smart disable does.
- In the Clip-launch layout, right-click a track's title and choose `Deactivate Track` to unload its devices until you need them again.

See also [Keyboard shortcuts](/wiki/shortcuts/).
