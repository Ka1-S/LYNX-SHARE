## The Channel rack

![The Channel rack: one row per sound, one square per step](/assets/img/learn/pattern-tour-1.webp)

1. On the welcome card, click `FL Studio`, or choose `View › Layout style › Pattern layout (default)`. The Channel rack (`F6`) shows the starter channels: Kick, Clap, Hat, Snare and Synth.
2. Each row is a sound and each square is a step (a 16th note). Click a square to add a hit, click it again to remove it.
3. A kick on steps 1, 5, 9 and 13, a clap on 5 and 13 and hats in between make a basic beat.
4. Press Play (or the space bar). In Pattern mode the pattern loops.

## The Piano roll

![Notes drawn in the Piano roll](/assets/img/learn/pattern-tour-2.webp)

1. Press `F7` to open the Piano roll of the selected channel.
2. Each row is a key of the keyboard on the left. Click in the grid to draw a note; drag its end to make it longer.
3. Right-click a note to delete it.

## The Playlist

![The pattern placed four times on a Playlist track](/assets/img/learn/pattern-tour-3.webp)

1. Press `F5` for the Playlist. It arranges your patterns into a song.
2. Click on a track to place the current pattern. Place it several times to make it play for longer.
3. Switch the transport from `Pattern` to `Song` (or press `L`) and press Play to hear the Playlist.

## The Mixer

![The Mixer with a track per channel](/assets/img/learn/pattern-tour-4.webp)

1. Press `F9` for the Mixer.
2. Each channel plays into a Mixer track. Drag a fader to set its level, and add effects in the track's slots.
3. The Master track on the left is what you hear and what you export.

## Good to know

- `F5`, `F6`, `F7` and `F9` show or hide the four windows, as in FL Studio. `F8` opens the Plugin picker.
- The same project opens in the Simple and Clip-launch layouts too: see [One project, three layouts](/learn/switch-layouts/).
