---
title: Move any knob with modulators
summary: Add an LFO, an envelope, steps or a macro in Tools › Modulators and assign it to any LYNX knob: the knob moves on its own while the song plays.
---
## Add a modulator

![Tools › Modulators with an LFO](/assets/img/learn/modulators-1.webp)

1. Choose `Tools › Modulators...`.
2. Click `+ Add modulator` and pick a kind: `LFO`, `Envelope follower`, `ADSR envelope`, `Steps`, `Random`, `Macro`, `Keytrack` or `Velocity`.
3. Set it up in its row: an LFO has a shape, a rate (synced to the tempo with `Sync`) and a phase.

## Assign it to a knob

![Assign mode: drag a knob to set the depth](/assets/img/learn/modulators-2.webp)

1. Click `Assign` in the modulator's row.
2. Drag any LYNX knob. How far you drag sets how deep the modulator moves it. Click `Assign` again to stop.
3. Or drag the row's grip onto a knob, or right-click a knob and choose `Modulate with`.

## Hear it

![The ring around the knob while it moves](/assets/img/learn/modulators-3.webp)

1. Press Play. A ring around the knob shows where the modulator takes it.
2. An automation clip on the same knob still sets the value the modulator moves around.

See also [Automation](/wiki/automation/).
