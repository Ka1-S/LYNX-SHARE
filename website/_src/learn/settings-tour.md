## The Settings pages

![The Settings window on the MIDI page](/assets/img/learn/settings-tour-1.webp)

1. Open Settings from the `Options` menu. `F10` opens them on the MIDI page, as in FL Studio; `Ctrl+,` opens them on the page you used last.
2. The pages follow FL Studio's order: `MIDI`, `Audio`, `General`, `Files`, `Theme`, `Project` and `Info`.
3. After a small gap come LYNX's own pages: `Ableton Link` and `LYNX extras`.

## Find a setting

![A match outlined by Find a setting](/assets/img/learn/settings-tour-2.webp)

1. Press `Ctrl+F` in the Settings window, or click the find box along its bottom, and type a few words, such as "metronome", "latency" or "autosave".
2. Settings jumps to the first match, on whichever page it is, and outlines it. `Enter` or `F3` goes to the next match, `Shift+Enter` back; the tabs without a match dim.

## Reset this page

![Reset this page and its confirmation](/assets/img/learn/settings-tour-3.webp)

1. Changed something you don't like? `Reset this page` puts every option of the page you are on back to its default, after you confirm.
2. For a few seconds after a reset, `Undo` brings your settings back.
