---
title: Edit one note inside an audio chord
summary: The polyphonic editor finds every note of an audio clip, chords included, so you can move one note of a recorded chord without recording again.
---
## Open the editor

![The audio clip's menu: Edit notes (polyphonic)...](/assets/img/learn/poly-editor-1.webp)

1. Right-click an audio clip in the Playlist and choose `Edit notes (polyphonic)...`.
2. The first time, LYNX SHARE analyses the clip in the background, with a progress bar and `Cancel`. The notes are saved with the project, so it never analyses the same clip twice.

## Move a note

![Every note of the chords drawn as a blob](/assets/img/learn/poly-editor-2.webp)

1. Each note is a blob on its own row, chords stacked, over a keyboard.
2. Drag a note up or down to change its pitch by semitones (hold `Alt` for cents), or left and right to move it in time.
3. Shift+click adds notes to the selection. The `Level` slider sets their level, `Del` mutes them and `R` resets them.
4. Edited notes turn orange, with their old place outlined.

## Listen

![The chord with the moved note](/assets/img/learn/poly-editor-3.webp)

1. `Play` in the editor plays the edited clip; `Original` plays it as it was.
2. Each edit is one undo step of the song, and the clip plays the edited audio in the Playlist.

## Good to know

- The tools at the top add pitch modulation, pitch drift and drawn pitch lines, as in Melodyne.
- With Melodyne installed, its ARA editor opens on the same clips (Windows and macOS).
