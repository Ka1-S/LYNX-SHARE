## The Mixer

![The Mixer with one strip per track](/assets/img/learn/mixing-basics-1.webp)

1. Open the Mixer with `F9`. Each track has a strip: name, pan, fader, mute, solo and meter.
2. The Master strip, on the left, is what you hear and what an export writes.

## Level and pan

![A fader pulled down](/assets/img/learn/mixing-basics-2.webp)

1. Drag a fader to set the track's level. Set the levels first, before any effect.
2. The knob above the fader pans the track left or right.

## Mute, solo and the meter

![Solo on one track](/assets/img/learn/mixing-basics-3.webp)

1. The green switch mutes a track; click it again to bring it back.
2. `S` solos the track: you hear only it. Click it again to hear everything.
3. Watch the Master meter: keep the loudest peaks out of the red.

See also [Mixer](/wiki/mixer/).
