## The shortcut sheet

![The shortcut sheet](/assets/img/learn/shortcuts-keys-1.webp)

1. Press `F1` (or `Help › Shortcuts`): the keys of every window, FL Studio's own in the Pattern layout. `F1` again closes it. (In FL Studio `F1` opens the manual; in LYNX SHARE the manual is `Help › Manual (wiki)...`.)

## Change a shortcut

![Manage shortcuts](/assets/img/learn/shortcuts-keys-2.webp)

1. Click `Manage shortcuts...` at the bottom of the sheet, or open `Options › Manage shortcuts...`.
2. Search an action or a key, select it, click `Change` and press the new keys.
3. `Changed only` lists your changes; `Reset to default` puts one shortcut back.

## The typing keyboard

![The Piano roll playing the typing keyboard's notes](/assets/img/learn/shortcuts-keys-3.webp)

1. `Ctrl+T` turns the typing keyboard on or off (`Options › Typing keyboard to piano`).
2. The letter keys play the selected instrument: Z to M is one octave, Q to I the next.

See also [Keyboard shortcuts](/wiki/shortcuts/).
