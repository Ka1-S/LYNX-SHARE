## Bring in the vocal

![A vocal take dragged onto the timeline](/assets/img/learn/vocal-chain-1.webp)

1. Record your voice on a Voice track, or drag a recording from your folders onto the timeline.
2. Raw vocals need a few steps to sound finished. LYNX has them in one menu.

## Clean voice

![The Clean voice chain on the track](/assets/img/learn/vocal-chain-2.webp)

1. Right-click the track and choose `Clean voice`, then `Clean voice with noise reduction`.
2. That adds a chain already set for a voice: noise reduction, EQ, gate, the Hush de-esser (it softens hard S sounds) and a compressor.

## Tune and reverb

![LYNX Tune and the Reverb knob](/assets/img/learn/vocal-chain-3.webp)

1. For pitch, click `+` in the Effects tab, `More effects...`, then `LYNX Tune`. It follows the song's key.
2. Turn the `Reverb` knob up a little, so the voice sits in the same room as the music.

See also [Recording](/wiki/recording/).
