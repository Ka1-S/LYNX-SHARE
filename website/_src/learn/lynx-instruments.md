## Load LYNX Model

![LYNX Model's window](/assets/img/learn/lynx-instruments-1.webp)

1. Choose `Add` and pick `LYNX Model` for a new channel, or right-click a channel in the Channel rack to replace its instrument. The Plugin picker (`F8`) lists it too.
2. Click the channel's name to open its window.
3. LYNX Model plays physical models, not samples: marimba, vibraphone, xylophone, glockenspiel, tubular bells and kalimba. Click one in the row at the top.

## Shape the sound

![The strike point on the bar](/assets/img/learn/lynx-instruments-2.webp)

1. Drag the strike point on the bar to change where the mallet hits it.
2. The `Mallet` knobs set its hardness, position, velocity and noise; the `Bar` knobs (`Tube`, `Tine`) its damping, resonator, motor and release; `Output` the tone and volume.
3. Every knob can be automated or moved by a modulator.

## Helix, the wavetable synth

![Helix's window](/assets/img/learn/lynx-instruments-3.webp)

1. Load `Helix` the same way.
2. Its oscillators play wavetables: turn the position knob to sweep through the waves of the table.
3. Filters, a modulation panel and the global column shape the rest. In the Clip-launch layout Helix shows as a card in the Device view.

## More of LYNX's own instruments

- `Tetra` is an FM synth and `Braid` a two-engine macro synth. They are in the same `Add` menu as every other instrument.
