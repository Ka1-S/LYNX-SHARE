## Theme presets

![The theme presets](/assets/img/learn/make-it-yours-1.webp)

1. Open `Options › Theme (colors, background)...`.
2. Pick a preset: LYNX, Ocean, Emerald, Amber, Violet or Graphite. `Default` brings LYNX's own back.

## Colours and text size

![Text size and Bold text on the General page](/assets/img/learn/make-it-yours-2.webp)

1. The knobs shift the hue, saturation, brightness and contrast; each dot colours one part, such as the tracks or the meters.
2. On the `General` page, `Text size` and `Bold text` make the interface easier to read.

## Language and restart

![LYNX after a restart, with bigger, bold text](/assets/img/learn/make-it-yours-3.webp)

1. `Language` lists the fifteen languages of LYNX SHARE.
2. A new language, font, text size or bold text applies when LYNX SHARE starts again.

See also [Settings](/wiki/settings/).
