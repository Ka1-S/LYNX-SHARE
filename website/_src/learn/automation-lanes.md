---
title: Automation lanes under your tracks
summary: Draw a parameter's curve right under its Playlist track, as in Live: turn on the lanes, add one with +A, click points, and play.
---
## Turn the lanes on

![The Playlist menu, Automation lanes](/assets/img/learn/automation-lanes-1.webp)

1. Open the Playlist's menu (the arrow at its top left) and choose `Automation lanes`, or switch it on in `Options › LYNX extras...`.

## Add a lane and draw

![A volume lane under the Chords track](/assets/img/learn/automation-lanes-2.webp)

1. Point at a track's name and click `+A`: pick what to automate, such as the track's volume or pan, the song's tempo, or the last control you moved.
2. Click in the lane to add points. Drag a point to move it, right-click a point to delete it, and `Alt`+drag a line to bend it.
3. The track's arrow folds its lanes away.

## Hear it

![Song mode playing the curve](/assets/img/learn/automation-lanes-3.webp)

1. Play the song: the parameter follows the curve.
2. FL Studio's automation clips keep working, and `Automation clips as lanes under their tracks` in the LYNX extras draws them in the same place. Any control's right-click menu has `Automate in lane`.

See also [Automation](/wiki/automation/) and [Automate a knob](/learn/automation-basics/).
