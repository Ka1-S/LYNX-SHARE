title: Record your voice

## Before you start

- Plug in a microphone or a headset, and choose it in `Options › Audio settings...` as the input device.
- Wear headphones, so the microphone does not pick up the playback.

## Add a Voice track

![The New track chooser with Voice / audio first](/assets/img/learn/record-voice-1.webp)

1. In the Simple layout, click `+ Add track`.
2. Click `Voice / audio`. A Voice track appears under the others, armed to record from the first input of your audio device.
3. The dock under the timeline shows the track, with its `Input Level` meter on the right. Sing a note and watch it move.

The `+ Voice track` button of the `Next: 3 things to try` card does the same.

## Record a take

![The Recording window: what would you like to record?](/assets/img/learn/record-voice-2.webp)

1. Press Record in the toolbar.
2. The first time, a window asks what to record. Choose `Audio, into the Playlist as an audio clip`. Tick `Don't ask again` to keep that choice.
3. Press Play, then sing or speak. The take is drawn on the Voice track while you record.
4. Press Stop. The take is now an audio clip on the timeline.
5. Press Record once more to switch recording off, so the next Play only plays.

![The take on the Voice track](/assets/img/learn/record-voice-3.webp)

## Add an effect and listen

![The one-knob effects of the dock](/assets/img/learn/record-voice-4.webp)

1. In the dock, click `+` and pick one of the one-knob effects. `Room` puts your voice in a room, `Echo` adds a tempo-synced echo and `Warm` adds gentle saturation.
2. Turn the effect's knob to set how much of it you hear. `Editor` opens all of its controls.
3. Press Play to hear your voice with the beat.

![The Room effect on the Voice track](/assets/img/learn/record-voice-5.webp)

## Good to know

- No sound coming in? Check the input device in `Options › Audio settings...`. A Voice track records from the device's first input.
- `Preset` above the effects loads a ready-made chain of effects for a voice.
- `Clean voice` in the track's menu reduces background noise.

See also [Recording](/wiki/recording/) and [Simple layout](/wiki/simple-layout/).
