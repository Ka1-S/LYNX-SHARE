Before you install

LYNX SHARE is an alpha. It opens, plays and exports whole projects, but it has bugs. Save often, and keep a copy of anything you import from another program.

Installed versions offer new releases through an Update bar under the toolbar. The Microsoft Store version updates through the Store, and the AppImage through AppImageUpdate or Gear Lever.

Plugin formats differ per system: VST3 and CLAP everywhere, Audio Units on macOS, LV2 on Linux, VST2 on Windows and Linux. ASIO and the 32-bit plugin bridge are Windows-only.
