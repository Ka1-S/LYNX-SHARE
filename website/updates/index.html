0.1.27alpha

2026-11-14Full notes on GitHub

Effects closer to the originals, more of FL Studio, Session mixer and Drum Rack, a first-song tour

LYNX SHARE's reverbs, delays, modulation effects, dynamics and voice tools were refitted against the plugins they are modelled on, and projects saved earlier keep their sound. More of FL Studio's windows and menus are in, the Clip-launch workflow gains a Session mixer, a Drum Rack and map modes, many of Live's own devices now open from .als sets, FL Studio Mobile projects gain DW Sampler and Slicer, and the Simple workflow gets an Effects tab and a first-song tour. LYNX SHARE also has a new logo, by w3teal.

New

  • Effects closer to the originals: Epoch Verb, Atrium, Foil Plate, Halo, Horizon and Nebula, Orbit, Spiral Echo, Lattice Mod, Gemini Doubler and Echoform have a measured law, and Sentry, Hush, Glow, Voice Shift and Quell gained controls measured on the plugins they are modelled on. Orbit's tail score went from 6.32 to 8.27 and Nebula's from 5.84 to 8.43 (out of 10). New instances use the measured law; projects and presets saved earlier load the classic one and sound exactly as before.
  • Remote control settings, as in FL Studio: right-click a control › Link to controller... opens the window with the port, channel, controller, the mapping formula, smoothing and FL's Mode list, for knobs, notes and buttons. Automation clips have FL's 13 curve modes per point, and the Piano roll gains Limit, Scale levels, Flip and Randomize tools.
  • Edison on a mixer slot, as in FL Studio: it records now, on input or on play, keeps recording with its window closed and comes in from FL projects with its audio. Tools › One-click audio recording records into Edison or the Playlist.
  • The Sampler page in FL Studio's layout: Content, Playback with loop points, ping pong and start offset, Time stretching, Precomputed effects with crossfade and trim, and the waveform. LYNX SHARE's own controls are in a last box. (#156)
  • Session mixer and Drum Rack (Clip-launch workflow): under the clip grid each track has its input, sends, track delay and volume, as in Live. A Drum Rack has 16 pads, choke groups and return chains; Live sets bring theirs in as one Drum Rack, and .als export writes it.
  • Scenes, Draw Mode and map modes (Clip-launch workflow): duplicate, capture and insert scenes, give a scene its own tempo and time signature, record into clip slots, draw automation in steps (B), and link MIDI controls or computer keys to clips, scenes and knobs with MIDI and Key Map Modes (Ctrl+M / Ctrl+K). Browser colour collections and hot-swap (Q) are there too.
  • Live's own devices open from .als sets as LYNX SHARE's own, with their settings and automation: EQ Eight, EQ Three, Compressor, Glue Compressor, Multiband Dynamics, Limiter, Gate, Utility, Reverb, Delay, Echo, Auto Filter, Saturator, Drum Buss and more, plus Simpler, Drift and Operator without FM. Several were fitted to Live's own renders.
  • FL Studio Mobile (.flm): DW Sampler and Slicer play on FL Studio Mobile's own engines, nine more effects come in on its own engines, instrument, pitch bend and controller lanes become automation, and chord sets and the key locker become the project's chords and key.
  • Simple workflow: a track dock with an Effects tab (eight one-knob effects, 22 chain presets and a Blend knob), an Instrument tab, a Keyboard tab with Scale keys and a Chord pad, and an Editor tab with the Piano roll. Loops follow the project key. A Welcome screen offers a seven-step first-song tour and two new demo songs, Late Bus and Sunrise.
  • Lyrics and notes, Reference match and Clean voice (in every layout): write lyrics beside the Playlist, match the Master's tone and loudness to a reference track, and put a ready voice chain on a track.

Better

  • Apex Limiter: with True peak on, the output now stays under the ceiling at every oversampling setting (it could go up to 2.85 dB over), and the true-peak meters read within 0.03 dB. The Finish dock's Master looks now stay under −1 dBTP.
  • Playback as in FL Studio: a later clip of the same pattern takes over from an earlier one (#158), and when two notes of the same key overlap, each note's end stops only its own voice.
  • FL Studio menus: the audio clip menu in FL's two columns, the channel's Levels adjustment (read from and written to .flp), Track mode › Audio track with the list of mixer tracks (thanks to scottholdren), instrument tracks, the multilink menu, plugin A/B states and more of FL's settings.
  • Gross Beat slots can be triggered by MIDI notes and its Smooth points follow FL's curve. Pitcher's MIDI mode and Harmonize take notes from its MIDI port.
  • Stand-ins: a project that uses a missing drum gate or a G3-style bus compressor opens with a closer LYNX SHARE effect.
  • New logo, by w3teal, in the app, the installers, the website and the Store.

Fixed

  • The metronome count-in and its length were not saved, a recording could land on another mixer insert than the one chosen, and a VST3 knob moved by a MIDI controller did not move in the plugin's window. (#152)
  • Spectral Compressor and other plugins with a sidechain input crashed LYNX SHARE. On Linux and macOS a plugin that crashes while it plays is now switched off and LYNX SHARE keeps going. (#155)
  • Searching for missing files, such as a SoundFont on a slow disk, could freeze the window. (#159)
  • After an edit, every Piano roll note of a Fruity Slicer played the same slice. (#160)
  • Find similar samples could wait a long time for an answer on a busy computer.

Known limits

  • .als export writes Drum Racks, but no Live has opened one yet. Live's devices with no LYNX SHARE equivalent (Hybrid Reverb, Grain Delay, Amp, Roar, FM Operator patches and others) are dropped on import and listed.
  • Some of Live's device settings follow assumed laws that have not been checked against Live yet, and a converted Live Reverb's wet signal is louder than Live's.
  • A 6/8 scene plays a three-beat bar.
  • The crash guard on Linux and macOS does not cover a plugin that crashes while opening its editor, loading a preset or on its own threads.
  • In FL Studio Mobile projects most DirectWave samples are not available, so most programs play a LYNX SHARE stand-in.

0.1.26alpha

2026-11-12Full notes on GitHub

Simple workflow, racks and Clip View, Slicex and FPC, freeze, controller profiles

A third layout, the Simple workflow, joins the Pattern and Clip-launch workflows. The Clip-launch side gains a Clip View, a Device View, racks, note chance and Live's MIDI tools. Slicex and FPC are instruments with FL Studio's editors, FL Studio Mobile projects come in with instruments, effects and automation, plugin windows dock, channels can be frozen and common MIDI controllers work from profiles.

New

  • Simple workflow (View › Layout style, and offered by the setup wizard): one window with the tracks and the timeline. The New track chooser adds a voice, an instrument, a sampler or a starter beat in one click, dropped loops keep time and the project has a key. The Finish dock gives the Master a ready-made sound and saves the song as WAV, MP3 or a package for other apps. The other windows stay one click away.
  • Clip View, Device View and racks (Clip-launch workflow): a selected clip opens with Clip, Launch and Notes panels and the Piano roll docked under the grid, and the selected track's effects show as a chain of cards. Instrument and Audio Effect Racks with chains, zones and macros open from Live sets and are written back, and a mixer slot's menu groups it into a rack. A MIDI Effect Rack can be built and is written to a Live set, but Live's own MIDI Effect Racks are not opened yet.
  • Note chance and MIDI tools: every note has a Chance and a Velocity range, and Live 12's probability groups are kept in .als sets. Live 12's MIDI tools (Arpeggiate, Chop, Connect, Ornament, Quantize, Recombine, Span, Strum, Time Warp and Velocity Shaper) are in the Clip View and in the Piano roll's Tools menu.
  • Warp markers: Auto-warp locks an audio clip to the beat, and markers can be added, moved and removed in the audio editor's warp lane. The Playlist shows them as flags on the clip.
  • Slicex and FPC are instruments with FL Studio's editor layout. FL's saved settings are read and written back, and both were compared with FL Studio's own renders. Fruity Slicer has its envelope page and voice settings, and a note-off ends its slice. (#74, #129, #135)
  • FL Studio Mobile (.flm): MiniSynth, Transistor Bass, 3x Osc and GMSynth channels play on LYNX SHARE's engines, FL Studio Mobile's effects come in as the new Pocket effects, and level, pan, mute, solo, pitch bend and send moves become automation clips. (#88)
  • Freeze (right-click a channel in the Channel rack): the channel plays audio rendered from it and its instrument is unloaded, a third-party plugin included. Volume, pan and the insert's effects stay live, and Unfreeze brings back exactly what was there.
  • Docked plugin windows, as in FL Studio: plugin editors sit inside the main window like the other panels. Settings › General has FL's "Detach all new plugins", and Tools › Macros › Switch all plugin editors to chooses Attached or Detached for all of them. A detached window can be pinned on top. (#116)
  • MIDI controller profiles (Settings › MIDI › Controller type): Mackie Control with its extender, Launchkey MK3, APC Key 25 mk2, KeyLab Essential mk3 and nanoKONTROL2, with soft takeover and feedback to the device. MPE, first stage: an MPE controller plays each note with its own bend, timbre and pressure, and a third-party plugin can receive MPE. (#108)
  • Online sessions: the host can share plugins with the guests. A shared plugin runs on the host's computer and a guest changes its settings from their own window, without owning it. A guest whose LYNX SHARE restarts goes back in as the same person, and Linux can host a session.
  • Projects: File › Versions... lists a project's saved versions and backups. A project can be opened without its third-party plugins, your own default template opens at start-up and with File › New (#123), and Export can write one file per marker section and keep named presets.
  • Tools: Convert to MIDI turns the melody, harmony or drums of an audio clip into a pattern, on your own computer. Piano roll scripts run FL Studio's .pyscript files (Tools › Scripts). The Browser finds samples similar to the one you right-click. Strike Shaper is a new transient designer (#94), and a loudness meter (LUFS and true peak) can sit beside the Master.

Better

  • Playlist, as in FL Studio: Edit and Select menus, Shift+arrow keys and Alt+Left / Right move clips (#147), fade and gain handles have menus with Create crossfade and Normalize, and there are Make unique as sample, Magic lasso and optional levels and controls on track headers. Pattern Blocks have pencil, brush and eraser tools (#127).
  • Mixer: deleting mixer tracks asks first, as in FL Studio. Links to a deleted insert can be kept and shown greyed, inserts can be arranged like the Playlist and a send can be pre-fader. A muted insert keeps its meter moving and keeps running its effects, as in FL Studio (#144).
  • Plugins: Processing › Connections sends an instrument's outputs to separate mixer inserts (#114). Third-party plugins in FL Studio projects play at FL's level (they were 3 dB quieter). A crashed plugin can be reloaded without reopening the project, LYNX SHARE's keys work while a plugin window has focus (#96), and VST3 parameter menus offer LYNX SHARE's automation items.
  • Piano roll: a Keyboard style option with traditional piano keys, a Chord stamp list, FL Studio's vertical zoom wheel, and Snap to scale moves notes by scale steps.
  • Recording and MIDI: sustain and sostenuto pedals hold notes, and a Layer passes the pedal on to its channels (#141). There are Tools › Cancel recording, an optional "Low latency while recording" switch and 16-bit disk recording.
  • Render to audio plays at the level of the insert it replaces (it was about 5 dB louder). Pattern renders and consolidated clips do too.
  • Effects: Crest, Sentry, Ember, Nebula, Halo, Foil Plate, Atrium, Epoch Verb, Echoform and Horizon were refitted against the tools they are modelled on. LYNX Tune has Breathiness (#103). When a project uses a third-party compressor, limiter, saturator or gate that is not installed, LYNX SHARE's stand-in loads with matching settings for more of them.
  • LYNX extras (Settings): one page with LYNX SHARE's own additions that are off by default, such as the loudness meter beside the Master and automation lanes under Playlist tracks. Preview in time (in the Browser's menu) and "Muted inserts don't process" (in the Mixer's menu) are new options too. The plugin database is now a folder you can arrange in the file manager (#104), and LYNX Dance loads sprite sheets made for Fruity Dance (#121).
  • Compressor has a FET voicing next to the clean one, and Apex Limiter has a levelling stage under the ceiling (off by default).

Fixed

  • A mixer track with a Patcher that holds a Fruity Limiter played about 1000 samples late compared with FL Studio. (#113)
  • Recording into a looping pattern clip folded the take over itself (#126), and an automation clip took a control back from a MIDI controller while you moved it (#125).
  • Changing the audio device while a project played could crash LYNX SHARE, and opening a Windows Audio device could take more than a minute on some devices. (#134)
  • Third-party plugins in imported FL Studio projects could be silenced by FL's cut groups. Surge XT was one. (#143)
  • A project opened over another one could keep the first project's settings for a plugin they both use.
  • An audio clip on a Sampler channel was silent, and an export could start before a SoundFont had finished loading (#138).
  • Linux: LYNX SHARE crashed at start on systems without a monospaced font (#142), and Linux could not host a live session. Samples in FL Studio's Shared data and Downloads folders are found (#124).
  • Mod X and Mod Y move the Sampler's filter, and a note's fine pitch reaches third-party plugins. (#63)

Known limits

  • Freeze works on instrument channels only, not on mixer inserts. The notes of a frozen channel cannot be edited until you Unfreeze it.
  • MPE is a first stage: one value of bend, timbre and pressure is recorded per note, not a curve. Of the built-in instruments, the Sampler, FPC and the built-in synth follow it, and the others do not yet. It has not been tried with a real MPE controller yet.
  • The controller profiles were written from each device's documentation and have not been tried on the hardware yet.
  • Harmless can play up to about 1.7 dB louder or quieter after a rest, depending on the audio buffer size.
  • LYNX Dance keeps Fruity Dance's "On desktop" option for FL Studio projects, but it does nothing: dancers stay in the main window.
  • .flm import: instruments other than MiniSynth, Transistor Bass, 3x Osc, GMSynth, DirectWave and the samplers play the built-in synth, and a .dwp missing from FL Studio's copy of the FLM resources plays a stand-in.
  • Live set export: LYNX SHARE's own instruments and effects are not converted (their tracks and notes are kept), and effect-parameter automation and insert-to-insert routes are dropped. On import, Live's own effects are dropped.
  • A plugin in a separate process, or in the 32-bit bridge, opens its editor in its own window and cannot be docked or pinned.

0.1.25alpha

2026-11-09Full notes on GitHub

Ableton Live sets, FL Studio Mobile import, 32-bit plugins

LYNX SHARE opens and exports Ableton Live sets (.als), imports FL Studio Mobile projects (.flm) and loads 32-bit plugins on Windows. It also adds video tracks with ADR, VCA faders, automation lanes and modulators, loop-take comping and an optional Clip-launch layout.

New

  • Ableton Live sets (.als): File › Open reads Live 11 and 12 sets, and File › Export › Ableton Live set (.als) writes them for Live 11 or 12 with the samples copied into the set's project folder. Import brings MIDI, audio, group and return tracks, tempo, locators, mixer settings with their automation and VST2 / VST3 plugins with their state; Simpler / Sampler and drum racks become LYNX SHARE samplers, and Live's other devices are listed in the import report.
  • FL Studio Mobile (.flm): File › Open imports the arrangement, samples, drum kits and DirectWave programs, with rack levels, pan and routes. Samples are searched next to the .flm and in FL Studio's copy of the FLM resources; missing ones go to the Missing files window.
  • 32-bit VST2 / VST3 plugins on Windows: they run through a bridge (LYNX-bridge32.exe) that adds no latency. A plugin that crashes in the bridge stops on its own while LYNX SHARE keeps playing.
  • Video tracks and ADR: video clips on the Playlist with a SMPTE ruler, frame-by-frame moves, Extract the sound and Export with video (needs FFmpeg). For dialogue replacement there is a cue list with streamers, beeps and take comping. (#83)
  • Clip-launch workflow (View › Layout style, optional): a clip grid with scenes next to the Playlist, with launch modes, quantisation, legato, loop and follow actions. Live sets bring their Session clips into it and export writes them back; the default Pattern workflow is unchanged.
  • VCA faders and folding mixer groups: Group › VCA › New VCA from selected makes a VCA that can be automated and nested. Groups fold to their bus strip, and any effect with a side input has a Sidechain from picker in one menu.
  • Comping: each pass of an audio loop recording lands on its own Playlist track, in a group. Comp these takes (right-click a track of the group) adds a comp track; drag across the take lanes to pick parts, then audition, crossfade and flatten.
  • Automation lanes and modulators: optional automation lanes under Playlist tracks (off by default), and Tools › Modulators... adds an LFO or random source that moves a knob around its own value. Multilink to controllers (Ctrl+J) links the controls you move to the controllers you move next, as in FL Studio.

Better

  • Mixer routing stays put: moving, inserting or deleting mixer tracks keeps sends, sidechains, Fruity Send, Transmitter, Patcher and Voltage Controller outputs, automation and MIDI links on their track.
  • Export to FL Studio: the exported project renders at the right length (FL's Thief demo came out at 8:12 instead of 3:59.9), and LYNX SHARE-only instruments rendered to audio play at unity in FL Studio (about 5 dB too loud before). Muted Playlist clips are written muted instead of being left out (#110), and clip fades are written so FL Studio plays them.
  • Stretched samples follow FL's TIME knob: precomputed effects and Trim are applied first, then only the span that plays is stretched. In FL's Thief demo the ride cymbal played about 3x too fast; projects that combine TIME with Trim, a precomputed fade or a Start / Length region may sound different from before.
  • Plugins in a separate process: this works on macOS too, and the plugin window does not float over other apps. Keys the plugin window does not use (Space, F-keys, your shortcuts) reach LYNX SHARE, and Esc closes the window. (#99)
  • Linux: LV2 plugins are scanned once in one helper process; with 510 plugins the first scan went from 25 minutes to about 12 s (#85), and a plugin that crashes the scan is blacklisted (#90). The interface font is Open Sans, bundled, because labels were cut short with wider distribution fonts; Settings › General › Interface font picks any installed font.

Fixed

  • A plugin crashing in its own window closed LYNX SHARE on Windows. A message offers Save as... and Always run in a separate process instead. (#32)
  • Third-party plugins with a side input get the slot's sidechain source, and the side input is on only while a source feeds it. Plugins such as RCompressor compress again. (#109)
  • Audio tracks lost their mixer link when a project was reopened. (#122)

Known limits

  • Render to audio (an insert bounced to an audio clip) plays about 5 dB louder than the insert it replaces. A fix is planned.
  • Live set export: LYNX SHARE's own instruments and effects are not converted (their tracks and notes are kept), effect-parameter automation and insert-to-insert routes are dropped, and Live trims overlapping audio clips. Live set import: racks keep their first chain, and Live's own effects are dropped.
  • .flm import: FLM's effects are not imported yet, and a .dwp missing from FL Studio's copy of the FLM resources plays a stand-in.
  • With several overlapping automation clips on one control, LYNX SHARE's choice can differ from FL Studio's. At a clip's start LYNX SHARE reaches the new value in about 8 ms, where FL Studio glides.
  • A 32-bit or bridged plugin's editor opens in its own window.
  • A tempo moved by a linked MIDI controller does not resize audio clips. Type or set the final tempo.

Export to FL Studio, live online sessions, plugin picker

A song can be exported as an FL Studio project, and online sessions are live, with reconnect, chat and permissions. Harmor and Sytrus have new editors, F8 opens a plugin picker with pictures, the toolbar was reworked and several of LYNX SHARE's own effects were refitted against the tools they are modelled on.

New

  • Export to FL Studio (File › Export › FL Studio project...): saves the song as an FL Studio 2026 project, either a .flp or a zipped loop package with every sample. Samplers, audio clips, FL's own generators and effects and VST2 / VST3 / CLAP plugins go over with their settings; anything only LYNX SHARE has is rendered to audio or swapped for the nearest FL plugin, and a report lists what happened to each part.
  • Live online sessions: a guest whose connection drops reconnects and keeps their edits, and if the host's LYNX SHARE closes, the next start offers to resume the session. There is a session chat, guests can follow the host's transport, the host can change what each person may edit, and parts that use a plugin someone does not have are rendered by the person who has it and streamed as audio.
  • Plugin picker (F8, as in FL Studio): every generator and effect shown as a picture of its own editor, with categories as tabs. Type to search, double-click or press Enter to add, or drag one onto the Channel rack or a mixer slot.
  • Pattern Blocks: FL's classic playlist, with one row per pattern next to the clip tracks. Paint with left-click, erase with right-click, and turn the blocks into clips when you are done. (#60)
  • Patcher parameter ports: right-click a node › Inputs / Outputs › Parameters, then drag a Surface knob onto a parameter to drive it live. This works for plugins too, and FL Patcher presets bring their ports along. (#65)
  • Capture MIDI (Ctrl+Shift+Alt+R): saves the last phrase you played without recording. Recording also keeps the chord played right on the downbeat after the count-in, and MIDI notes are timed to the sample.

Better

  • Harmor and Sytrus editors: Harmor has a new editor in FL's section order, with part A and part B pages, a Global strip with the X / Y / Z pad and an FX panel; part B and the global settings can be edited. Sytrus shows FL's FM / RM modulation matrix.
  • Toolbar: bigger icons and buttons, a large song position, master volume and pitch as two knobs, and a magnet to switch snap on and off. The recording switches sit side by side, with new Loop record, Step edit (Ctrl+E), Punch in/out, Blend (Ctrl+B) and a tempo tapper.
  • Mixer and plugin menus: several selected mixer tracks can be routed at once, effect slots are fixed places as FL's 10 slots are, and plugin menus are listed A to Z under FL's categories, where a letter key jumps to the next plugin starting with it (#64). Dragging a mixer fader during playback keeps up with the mouse instead of stalling the meters.
  • Effects closer to the originals: Apex Limiter, Crest Compressor, Sentry Gate, Hush De-esser and Prism Multiband were refitted; LuxeVerb gains a Space page, Ridge Strip a Modern console voicing and Ember Saturator a Classic panel. Aether's Mid Air is audible again (#77).
  • FL generators and projects: every new FL generator starts like a new one in FL Studio when FL Studio is installed. Transistor Bass, FL Slayer, BooBass and DrumSynth Live accept FL 2025's files too, and Drumaxx, FPC, Slicex and Transistor Bass play at FL's level.

Fixed

  • VST3 instruments cut chords off. Each note carries its own note ID, as FL Studio does. (#70)
  • Parametric EQ lost FL presets after save and reopen. Every setting of every built-in effect is saved exactly, inside Patcher too. (#67)
  • Detached windows come back detached, at their own place and size. (#62)
  • Linux file choosers: when the system dialog cannot open, LYNX SHARE uses its own file chooser, and upper-case extensions are listed. (#72)
  • Notes were left hanging after deleting, moving or lengthening them while they play, and Playlist clicks could land about two bars early. (#32)

Project packages for friends, designed plugin editors, Microsoft Store

A project can be sent to a friend as one file and their part merged back. Nearly 40 FL plugins that opened as a plain knob grid have editors laid out like FL's, the Mixer has FL's eight layouts and track icons, and LYNX SHARE is on the Microsoft Store.

New

  • Send a project to a friend (File › Online session): the project travels as one .lynxpack file with every sample and audio renders of the parts that use third-party plugins. Your friend records their part and uses Send my part back..., you add it with Merge a part..., and one Undo takes it out again; up to 4 people per project, no account or server needed. The live version is not in this release.
  • Microsoft Store: LYNX SHARE is available on the Microsoft Store for Windows. The Store version updates through the Store.
  • Mixer layouts and track icons: FL's eight layouts in the Mixer's title bar, from Compact to Extra large, kept per user. Mixer tracks, channels and Playlist tracks can have an icon (Change icon...), and FL projects keep theirs. (#52)
  • Record an insert after its effects: FL's six Record positions in the insert's input menu (Post EQ by default), with the effects' latency taken out. The dry input can also be recorded as a second take, for re-amping. (#41)
  • Render and export: Patterns › Quick render, Render as audio clip and Render and replace, Consolidate playlist selection (Ctrl+Alt+C), Export all playlist tracks, Project bones and Export › Master... (Ctrl+Alt+M), which masters to a loudness target on your computer.
  • LYNX SHARE preset banks: Toxic Biohazard, PoiZone, Harmless, DX10, SimSynth, BeepMap, Granulizer, Fruit Kick, Drumpad, Sakura and others have presets that work without FL Studio. The preset arrows step through LYNX SHARE's presets, your own, then FL's when FL Studio is installed.

Better

  • Designed plugin editors: Toxic Biohazard, PoiZone, Harmless, Ogun, GMS, Morphine, Kepler, Transistor Bass, Sawer, Sakura, FL Slayer, Love Philter, Peak Controller and about 25 more open in FL's own layout, drawn in LYNX SHARE's style. LYNX SHARE's own effects (EQ, Delay, Reverb, Limiter and more) show what the setting does, such as the frequency response or the echoes on the beat grid.
  • Maximus In/Out graph can be edited with FL's gestures: right-click to add a point, drag points and curve handles, Alt+right-click to delete. It is saved in FL's format and the sound follows the curve. (#40)
  • Typing keyboard to piano is in Options (Ctrl+T) under FL's name, on by default and at FL's octave. Right-click its button for Root note and Octave. (#34)
  • Playlist and Piano roll: Alt+M / Shift+Alt+M mute and unmute the selected clips (#53), and a STRETCH button (Shift+M) makes resizing an audio clip stretch it. The Piano roll gets note grid highlights (Alt+B), a background waveform (Alt+N), FL score files (.fsc) and the MIDI clipboard shared with FL Studio.
  • Linux AppImage updates in place with Gear Lever or AppImageUpdate. (#48)

Fixed

  • macOS: LYNX SHARE closed a few seconds after launch when a folder in the Browser held a file name macOS cannot read as text. (#42)
  • u-he CLAP plugins (Presswerk) crashed LYNX SHARE after asking for a restart, also during export. (#46)
  • Moving a mixer effect slot, track or channel reset the plugin's settings, and reordering slots with Presswerk crashed on Linux. (#36)
  • Multi-monitor menus: submenus stay on the menu's own display, and on Linux menus in the main window open again while a panel is undocked. (#45)
  • With the loop region on, each pass played one sample too many, so long loops drifted off the beat. Fit to tempo also keeps a loop's exact length instead of stretching it to a power of two. (#32)

0.1.22alpha

2026-11-03Full notes on GitHub

Stem extraction, looping pattern clips, FL's MIDI Out

Extract stems from sample splits an audio clip into drums, bass, instruments and vocals on your own computer. Stretched pattern clips loop their pattern, Song-mode recording keeps every bar, MIDI Out works like FL's, and FL projects open closer to how they were saved.

New

  • Extract stems from sample (Playlist, audio clip menu): splits the clip's sample into drums, bass, instruments and vocals, each on a new track under the clip. It uses the HT-Demucs model (166 MB), downloaded on first use only if you agree; your audio never leaves your computer, and a 3-minute song takes about 2.5 minutes on a 6-core PC with about 4 GB of memory at peak. (#31)
  • SFZ instruments play in the LYNX Soundfont channel, with round robin, release triggers, choke groups, key switches and loops. Samples can be WAV, FLAC, OGG, AIFF or MP3. (#22)
  • Gross Beat with slots, presets and an editable envelope: a LYNX SHARE factory bank (25 time and 30 volume slots, 29 presets) and FL's envelope gestures. Edits are heard right away and saved in FL's Gross Beat format.
  • Mono, Porta and Slide on every instrument (Channel settings › Misc): FL engines, the drums, the Sampler and VST / VST3 / CLAP plugins. Imported FL projects keep these settings.
