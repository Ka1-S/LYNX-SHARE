Play LYNX with a MIDI keyboard

Plug in a MIDI keyboard, check it in Options › MIDI settings, play any instrument and record your part into the Piano roll.

Any layout · 0:50 · Beginner

Chapters

MIDI settings

  1. Plug in your keyboard (USB or a MIDI interface), then choose Options › MIDI settings... (F10).
  2. Your keyboard is listed under Input devices and switched on. It listens to any MIDI channel unless you pick one.
  3. It plays the selected channels of the Channel rack, or always the channel you choose.

Play an instrument

  1. Select an instrument in the Channel rack and play: you hear it at once.
  2. No driver or setup is needed: class-compliant keyboards just work.

Record notes

  1. Press Record, choose Notes and automation, then press Play and play your part.
  2. Stop: the notes are in the Piano roll, ready to edit and quantize.

See also MIDI controllers.
