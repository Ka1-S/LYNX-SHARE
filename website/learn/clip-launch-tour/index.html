Tour the Clip-launch layout

The Ableton Live way in LYNX SHARE: a grid of clips, scenes made from the song's sections, launching, the Device view and the arrangement.

Clip-launch layout · 1:00 · Beginner

Chapters

The grid and the arrangement

  1. On the welcome card, click Ableton Live or Bitwig, or choose View › Layout style › Clip-launch layout.
  2. The Playlist's tracks are the columns of the clip grid; each row is a scene. The arrangement sits on the right.
  3. Here we open LYNX's demo song. Its arrangement has three sections: Intro, Verse and Hook.

Scenes from sections

  1. Right-click a slot and choose Create scenes from sections.
  2. Each section becomes a scene, named and coloured after it, with every track's clip of that section.

Launch scenes and clips

  1. Click the launch button at the end of a scene's row: its clips start together.
  2. Launch another scene. It waits for the next bar, then takes over.
  3. Click one clip's launch button to start just that track's clip.
  4. Stop all clips with the stop button of the Main column, or press Stop.

Devices and the arrangement

  1. View › Device view (Ctrl+Alt+4) shows the selected track's instrument and effects under the grid. Ctrl+Alt+3 shows the Clip view.
  2. Song shows only the arrangement, Grid only the clip grid, Both the two side by side.
  3. A track that launched a clip leaves the arrangement until you click Back to Playlist.
