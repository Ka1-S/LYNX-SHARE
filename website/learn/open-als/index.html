Open an Ableton Live set

Open a .als in the Clip-launch layout: tracks, clips, scenes, the arrangement and Live's colours come over, and you can export back to Live.

Clip-launch layout · 0:45 · Beginner

Chapters

Open the .als

  1. Choose File › Open... (Ctrl+O) and pick the set's .als file, inside its project folder.
  2. LYNX SHARE opens it in the Clip-launch layout. Ableton Live itself is not needed, and the .als is never changed.

What comes over

  • MIDI, audio, group and return tracks, the tempo, the time signature, locators and the loop.
  • The arrangement, and session clips and scenes into the clip grid, with their launch settings.
  • The mixer: volume, pan, mute, solo, sends and their automation. Live's colours stay.
  • VST2 and VST3 plugins with their state, when the same plugin is installed.

Play and look at the devices

  1. Press Play to hear the arrangement, or launch clips and scenes in the grid.
  2. Click a track's title in the grid, then View › Device view (Ctrl+Alt+4) shows its devices.

Import report and export

  1. When something had to change, a report opens with the set. Help › Last project import report shows it again: what was approximated or left out.
  2. File › Export › Ableton Live set (.als)... writes a set for Live 12 or Live 11.
