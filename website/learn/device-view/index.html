The Device view

In the Clip-launch layout, the Device view shows a track's instrument and effects in a row, as in Live: add effects from the Browser, open a device's window, copy devices between tracks.

Clip-launch layout · 0:56 · Intermediate

Chapters

Show a track's devices

  1. In the Clip-launch layout, press Ctrl+Alt+4 to show the Device view under the grid.
  2. Click a track's title: its instrument comes first, then its effects, in the order the sound goes through them.

Add effects

  1. In the Browser, click Audio Effects and drag an effect onto the chain, where you want it.

Edit, copy, switch

  1. Each device shows its main knobs; its window button opens the full editor.
  2. Select a device and press Ctrl+C, then Ctrl+V on another track, to copy it.
  3. Shift+Tab switches between the Device view and the clip.

See also The three layouts and Tour the Clip-launch layout.
