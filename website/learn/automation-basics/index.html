Automate a knob

Right-click any knob for Create automation clip, shape its curve in the automation editor, and hear the knob move by itself; in the Simple layout, automation lanes.

Pattern layout · 0:50 · Beginner

Chapters

Create an automation clip

  1. Right-click any knob, here a synth's Cutoff, and choose Create automation clip.
  2. The clip goes into the Playlist, as long as the pattern, and a channel for it appears in the Channel rack.

Shape the curve

  1. The automation editor opens with the clip's curve. To open it later, right-click the knob and choose Edit automation clip....
  2. Drag the points to shape the curve; click to add a point. Here the Cutoff starts high and falls over the bar.

Hear it

  1. Press Play in Song mode: the knob follows the curve every time the song plays.
  2. In the Simple layout, the Automation button shows a lane under each track to draw in.

See also Automation.
