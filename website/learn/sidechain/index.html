Sidechain: duck the bass under the kick

Make room for the kick: Duck under in the Simple layout in one pick, or the Pattern layout's way with a sidechain route and a compressor in the Mixer.

Any layout · 1:00 · Intermediate

Chapters

What sidechain does

  1. Sidechain ducking dips a track on every hit of another one, a bass under the kick, so both are heard.

Duck under (Simple layout)

  1. Select the bass track and open its Effects tab.
  2. Click Duck under and pick Drums. The bass now breathes with the beat; the Duck card's knob sets how deep.

Mixer sidechain (Pattern layout)

  1. In the Mixer, select the Drums track and right-click the arrow under the chords: Sidechain to this track.
  2. Add a compressor to the chords' track: it listens to the drums and pushes the chords down on every hit.

See also Mixer.
