Build a song from loops

The Loops library in the Simple layout: find a loop, hear it, drag it onto the timeline. Loops play at the song's tempo and follow its key.

Simple layout · 0:54 · Beginner

Chapters

Find a loop

  1. In the Simple layout click Loops. The library lists LYNX's own sounds and beats and the audio loops in your folders (Music, Downloads and the folders you add).
  2. Type in the search box, or pick an instrument, a genre or a descriptor.

Hear it and add it

  1. Click a loop to hear it. LYNX reads its length, tempo and key, and shows them in the list.
  2. Drag it onto the timeline: it becomes a new track and plays at the song's tempo.
  3. If the song has no key yet, LYNX offers to set it to the loop's key: click Set the key.
  4. LYNX's own sounds and beats are added with a double-click.

Play and change the key

  1. Add more loops the same way and press Play: they play in time with the beat.
  2. Click Key and pick another key: loops that follow the key move to it and keep their length.
